* Existing Libraries and references

** Libraries

- dimcat: https://github.com/DCMLab/dimcat
  - also uses collection-of-dataframes representation
    - has mechanisms for loading DFs at once
  - fixed column layout (specialized to MuseScore + harmony files)
  - no general interval relations or operations
- CAMAT: https://github.com/egorpol/camat_v2
  - dataframes, but two specific (notes, events)
  - fixed columns, specialized to MEI
  - no general interval relations and operations
- music21: https://music21.org/
  - modeled after notation / MusicXML
  - graph of objects representation
- partitura: https://github.com/CPJKU/partitura
  - notation-oriented (MusicXML) + feature extraction for MIR\ML
  - graph-of-objects
  - events aligned on time axis
    - => some time-related operations
    - part is series of timepoints with pointers to beginning/ending objects
    - different object types on same axis (per part)
- humdrumR: https://github.com/Computational-Cognitive-Musicology-Lab/humdrumR/
  - DF representation (1 DF for all)
  - humdrum-specific - focussed on tokens and their properties
